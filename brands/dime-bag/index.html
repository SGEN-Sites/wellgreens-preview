The Story

Dime Bag is all about cannabis flower, giving Wellgreens shoppers a broad strain selection without overcomplicating the experience. The lineup stretches across Sativa, Indica, and Hybrid options, with names including Blue Dream, Toronja, Zonuts, Planetary OG, Papaya Bomb, Aspen Dreamz, Donny Burger, Permanent Marker, and more. Most of the assortment is represented in 3.5g / 1/8 oz flour, making it easy to browse different strains in a familiar format. For customers who like having plenty of choices, Dime Bag brings serious variety to the Wellgreens flower menu. The selection also reaches into 14g / 1/2 oz cannabis flower, with strains such as Black Magic, Blue Dream, Zonuts, BTY OG, Durban Haze, Gelatti, Amaretto, Toronja, Island Honey, and Colombian Red represented in the larger format. That gives shoppers the flexibility to explore both standard eighths and larger flower quantities depending on what's available at their preferred Wellgreens dispensary. With Dime Bag represented across all 10 stores, the lineup covers a wide range of strains and sizes throughout San Diego County.

FAQ

Frequently Asked

01What kind of cannabis products does Dime Bag offer at Wellgreens?

The current Dime Bag selection at Wellgreens is focused entirely on cannabis flowers. The lineup includes both 3.5g / 1/8 oz and 14g / 1/2 oz sizes, depending on the strain and store. Shoppers can browse Sativa, Indica, and Hybrid selections across the brand's current assortment.

02What Dime Bag flower strains can I find at Wellgreens?

There is a wide range of strains represented across the Wellgreens locations carrying Dime Bag. Current selections include Blue Dream, Zonuts, Toronja, Planetary OG, Papaya Bomb, Durban Haze, Donny Burger, Permanent Marker, Hindu Kush, Strawberry Daiquiri, and many more. Not every strain is stocked at every location, so check your preferred store's current cannabis menu when you're looking for a particular option.

03Does Dime Bag come in larger flower sizes?

Yes. Along with its extensive 3.5g flower selection, Dime Bag has 14g / 1/2 oz cannabis flowers represented at Mission Valley, Pacific Beach, and Vista. Current larger-format strains include options such as Black Magic, Blue Dream, Zonuts, BTY OG, Gelatti, Street Guru, Amaretto, Toronja, Island Honey, and Colombian Red. Availability varies, so larger sizes may not appear at every Wellgreens dispensary.

04Can I find Sativa, Indica, and Hybrid Dime Bag flowers?

Yes. The current Dime Bag assortment spans all three classifications as displayed in the Wellgreens listings. Blue Dream and Toronja are examples of Sativa options, Planetary OG and Papaya Bomb are displayed as Indica, while Zonuts and Amaretto are displayed as Hybrid. Since individual strains can vary, review the current product details when choosing your flower.

05Is Dime Bag available at every Wellgreens location?

Dime Bag is represented at all 10 Wellgreens dispensaries in the current assortment. That includes locations throughout Chula Vista, San Diego, Encinitas, La Mesa, Lemon Grove, and Vista. Each store has its own strain and size selection, so browsing the current menu is the easiest way to see what's available near you.
