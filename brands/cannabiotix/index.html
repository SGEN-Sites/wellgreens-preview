The Story

Two Santa Monica friends who started growing in underground Southern California bedrooms in the early 2000s, launched legally in Nevada's medical market, and returned to Los Angeles in 2017 to become one of California's most decorated flower brands. They control a 55+ strain genetic library bred in-house, cultivate PGR-free with vacuum-sealing post-test, and sit at or near the top of California flower sales charts through 2024-2025.

  • 20+ High Times Cannabis Cup wins, High Times Top 100 founder recognition, in-house breeding with decades of phenotype selection, PGR-free cultivation, vacuum-sealing for terpene preservation, direct-to-consumer CBX Club delivery program, sister-brand ecosystem (Highatus, Heirbloom)

FAQ

Frequently Asked

01What is Cannabiotix?

Cannabiotix (stylized CBX) is a premium, award-winning boutique California cannabis brand founded in 2014 in Southern California by Neema "Neem the Dream" Samari and J.B. "Bones." Its one-liner is "All We Know Is Heat"premium flower and concentrates built on decades of in-house genetic curation.

02What Cannabiotix products are available at Wellgreens?

Wellgreens Pacific Beach stocks 41 CBX Products including 3.5g, 7g, and 14g flower (Cereal Milk, Kush Mountains, Casino Kush, Tropicanna, Jet Lag OG), 0.75g single and 2g 4-pack pre-rolls, 0.5g vape cartridges (including Live Resin), Terp Sugar concentrates, and Tier 1 / Tier 2 Cold Cure Live Rosin from the CBX Club solventless line.

03What makes Cannabiotix different?

CBX holds 20+ High Times Cannabis Cup wins, a 55+ strain in-house genetics library, PGR-free cultivation, and vacuum-sealing post-test for terpene preservation. It also runs a direct-to-consumer CBX Club delivery program and operates sister brands Highatus and Heirbloom.

04What's Cannabiotix's signature product?

Cereal Milk (Y Life × Snowman) is CBX's flagship hybrid, it moved 21k+ California units as a 3.5g in January 2025 per Headset. Other signature strains include Kush Mountains, Tropicanna, Master Kush, White Walker OG, and Jet Lag OG.

05Has Cannabiotix won any awards?

CBX has 20+ High Times Cannabis Cup wins including 2017 Top Ten Strain of the Year (Gluetopia), 2017 World Cup 1st Best Hybrid Flower (Kush Mountains), 2016 LA HT Cup 1st Best Indica (Master Kush), 2015 Jamaica World Cup 1st Best Indica (White Walker OG) plus Trailblazer Award, and 2013 Amsterdam HT Cup 1st Best Indica. Founders Neema and JB are named in High Times Top 100 Most Influential People in Cannabis, and CBX was the #1 California flower brand in Dec 2024 and Jan 2025 per Headset.
