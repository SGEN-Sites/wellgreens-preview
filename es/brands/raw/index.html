La historia

RAW gives cannabis flower shoppers a broad selection of rolling papers, pre-rolled cones, tips, and rolling accessories at Wellgreens. Paper formats range from 1 1/4 and Single Wide to King Size Wide and Black King Size Slim, while the cone selection includes Classic, Classic Black, Organic, and 120mm Stogee options. Shoppers can also find Organic Hemp papers for a different paper choice within the lineup. With several sizes and formats represented, RAW makes it easy to shop based on how you prefer to prepare and roll flowers. The collection goes beyond papers with rolling tips, pre-rolled tips, and rolling machines in multiple sizes. Select stores also carry products such as Connoisseur papers with tips, Artesano Papers & Tips, Hydrostone, and Lyrical Lemonade Cones. That mix gives RAW a strong accessories presence at Wellgreens, whether you're restocking everyday rolling supplies or looking for a different paper, cone, or rolling format.

FAQ

Preguntas frecuentes

01What kinds of RAW rolling papers can I find at Wellgreens?

The RAW selection includes several paper sizes and styles rather than a single standard format. Shoppers can find 1 1/4 Size Papers, Organic Hemp 1 1/4 Papers, Single Wide papers, King Size Wide, and Black King Size Slim. Select stores also carry Connoisseur and Artesano paper-and-tip options. With that range, you can browse based on paper size and format instead of settling for one choice.

02Do Wellgreens carry RAW cones?

Yes. RAW cones are a substantial part of the collection, including Classic 1 1/4, Classic Black 1 1/4, and Organic 1 1/4 Cones in 6-packs. Select locations also carry 120mm Stogee Cones in 3-packs and Lyrical Lemonade Cones in 2-count packs. This gives cone shoppers several sizes and pack styles to explore depending on the store.

03Can I get RAW tips without buying rolling papers?

Absolutely. Wellgreens carries standalone RAW Tips in 20-count and 50-count options, along with pre-rolled tips in 21-count packs at select stores. There are also RAW products that pair papers and tips together if you'd rather pick up both in one item. Look for the exact format you want when browsing your local store's accessories.

04What sizes of RAW rolling machines are available?

RAW rolling machines appear in 70mm, 79mm, and 110mm sizes across the Wellgreens selection. Not every size is carried at every location, so you'll see some variation between stores. For example, Pacific Beach currently carries both 79mm and 110mm options.

05Where can I find the widest RAW selection at Wellgreens?

Wellgreens Encinitas currently has the largest RAW assortment among the participating stores, followed by Kearny Mesa, La Mesa Lake Murray, and Vista. Encinitas includes papers, cones, tips, rolling machines, and several specialty formats in its RAW lineup. If you're hunting for a particular size or accessory, checking your preferred store's current menu is the quickest way to narrow it down.
