La historia

Newport adds a focused selection of torch accessories and butane refill products to the accessories available at Wellgreens. The current lineup includes the Newport Pen Torch and Newport Gun Torch, giving shoppers two torch options to browse. Kix Butane Refill products round out the selection in 300 ML and 750 ML sizes. Together, these four products keep the collection straightforward and centered on torch and refill essentials. Across Wellgreens, the Newport Gun Torch is carried at several locations, while the Pen Torch is currently listed at Chula Vista. The Kix 300 ML Butane Refill has the widest availability in the collection, appearing at all nine stores that currently carry Newport-related products. A larger 750 ML Kix Butane Refill is also available at Vista. With Newport products carried from Chula Vista and San Diego to Encinitas, La Mesa, and Vista, shoppers can check their nearest participating Wellgreens for current accessory availability.

FAQ

Preguntas frecuentes

01What Newport products are available at Wellgreens?

The current Newport collection includes four products: Newport Pen Torch, Newport Gun Torch, Kix Butane Refill 300 ML, and Kix Butane Refill 750 ML. The assortment is focused on torch accessories and butane refill products rather than cannabis itself. Individual products are distributed differently across the nine participating stores. Check your preferred Wellgreens location to see which options are currently stocked.

02Which Wellgreens stores carry the Newport Gun Torch?

The Newport Gun Torch is currently listed at Encinitas, La Mesa Lake Murray, Mission Valley, Pacific Beach, and Vista. The product is not listed at every Wellgreens location carrying the broader Newport selection. The Newport Pen Torch is currently listed separately at Chula Vista. Availability can therefore depend on which torch model you are looking for.

03What sizes of Kix Butane Refill are available?

Wellgreens currently lists Kix Butane Refill products in 300 ML and 750 ML sizes. The 300 ML option has much broader availability and appears at all nine locations carrying the Newport collection. The 750 ML refill is currently listed at Wellgreens Vista. Shoppers looking for a particular size should check the corresponding store before visiting.

04Does every Wellgreens store carry the same Newport accessories?

No. Newport-related inventory varies across participating Wellgreens stores. Vista currently lists three products, while several locations list one or two. Some stores carry a Newport torch alongside Kix Butane Refill, while others currently list only the 300 ML refill. This makes the local Wellgreens menu the best way to check the available selection.

05Is the Kix 300 ML Butane Refill widely available at Wellgreens?

Yes. The Kix Butane Refill 300 ML is the most widely listed product in the current collection. It appears at all nine Wellgreens locations carrying Newport-related inventory, including Chula Vista, City Heights, Encinitas, Kearny Mesa, both participating La Mesa stores, Mission Valley, Pacific Beach, and Vista. The larger 750 ML size has more limited availability. This gives the 300 ML option the broadest store coverage in the collection.
