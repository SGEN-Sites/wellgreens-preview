La historia

Uncle Arnie's is firmly planted in the cannabis beverage category, but the selection goes well beyond one type of drink. At Wellgreens, you'll find 12oz drinks and sodas alongside 8oz live resin beverages, 2oz cannabis shots, and select 7.5oz drinks. The collection covers flavors ranging from Smacking Apple and Strawberry to Iced Tea Lemonade, Pineapple Paradise, Blackberry Lemonade, and Tropical Twist. Most products are labeled at 100mg, while select 7.5oz beverages are labeled at 10mg. The 2oz shot collection adds a different dimension with THC:CBN, THC:CBG, and THC:CBD formulas, alongside Hybrid-labeled options such as Magic Mango, Strawberry Kiwi, and Sunrise Orange. Wellgreens also carries 8oz live resin drinks such as Blackberry Lemonade, Raspberry Lemonade, Tropical Twist, and Pacific Surge across select stores. With nine participating Wellgreens locations, Uncle Arnie's gives cannabis beverage shoppers several formats and flavor styles to browse throughout San Diego County.

FAQ

Preguntas frecuentes

01Does Uncle Arnie's only make full-size cannabis drinks?

Not in the Wellgreens selection. We carry Uncle Arnie's in several formats, including 12oz drinks and sodas, 8oz live resin beverages, 2oz shots, and 7.5oz drinks. That means you can explore the brand without being locked into a single beverage size or style. The exact selection depends on which Wellgreens store you're shopping.

02Which Uncle Arnie's products are labeled at 10mg?

The current collection includes three 7.5oz drinks labeled at 10mg: Iced Tea Lemonade, Zen Green Tea, and Watermelon Wave. All three appear in the Encinitas selection, while Zen Green Tea is also listed at Pacific Beach. The rest of the products in the supplied Uncle Arnie's selection are labeled at 100mg. Always check the individual package for complete serving and cannabinoid information.

03What cannabinoid combinations are available in Uncle Arnie's shots?

Several 2oz shots call out specific cannabinoid combinations right in their product names. Blueberry Night Cap is labeled THC:CBN, Tahitian Trip is THC:CBG, and Lemon Mint is THC:CBD. The collection also includes Magic Mango, Strawberry Kiwi, and Sunrise Orange shots labeled Hybrid. That gives you more to compare than flavor alone when browsing Uncle Arnie's shots.

04Does Uncle Arnie's have live resin cannabis beverages?

Yes. The Wellgreens selection includes 8oz live resin drinks, including Blackberry Lemonade, Tropical Twist, Raspberry Lemonade, and Pacific Surge. Availability differs by store, so you may not see every flavor at every participating location. These products are labeled at 100mg in the current selection.

05How many Uncle Arnie's products does Wellgreens carry?

Across the combined Wellgreens selection, there are 18 different Uncle Arnie's products spanning several beverage formats. Those products are carried across nine stores, with the exact menu changing by location. The collection includes 10mg and 100mg beverages, sodas, 2oz shots, and live resin drinks. You can check your preferred Wellgreens menu when you're looking for a particular flavor or format.
