Our Services are intended exclusively for individuals who are 21 years of age or older, or 18 years of age with a valid medical cannabis recommendation or as a registered caregiver under applicable state law (collectively, "Permitted Users"). By using the Services, you affirm that you meet these age requirements and have the legal capacity to enter into this agreement.

We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from individuals under the age of 18, or under 21 without a valid medical recommendation. If you are under the minimum permitted age, do not use or provide any information through the Services, make purchases, or register for an account. Any such use is a direct violation of this Policy and the applicable Terms of Use.

We use Web beacons to manage cookies, count visits, and to learn what marketing works and what does not. We also use Web beacons to tell if you open or act on our emails.

Others, including third party analytics service providers and advertising partners may also collect personally identifiable information about your online activities over time and across different Web sites when you use our Services, including as described in this Privacy Policy. Privacy Rights for California Residents

California residents have specific privacy rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and other applicable laws. This section explains your rights as a California resident, how you can exercise those rights, and how Wellgreens complies with these legal requirements.

1. Right to Know

You have the right to request information about the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you over the past 12 months. This includes:

  • (a) The categories of personal information collected
  • (b) The categories of sources from which the information was collected
  • (c) The business or commercial purpose for collecting your information
  • (d) The categories of third parties with whom your information was shared

2. Right to Delete

You have the right to request the deletion of personal information we have collected from you, subject to certain legal exceptions. For example, we may retain information needed to:

  • (a) Complete transactions
  • (b) Detect and prevent fraudulent activity
  • (c) Comply with legal obligations

3. Right to Opt-Out of Sale

We do not sell your personal information for monetary gain. However, under California law, certain data-sharing practices may be considered a "sale." You have the right to opt-out of such data sharing.

4. Non-Discrimination

We will not discriminate against you for exercising your CCPA rights. This means we will not deny you services, charge different prices, or provide a different level of service based on your decision to exercise your privacy rights.
