Featured Cannabis Brands Available at Wellgreens

Our featured Wellgreens cannabis brands represent some of California’s most respected names in cannabis. Whether you’re searching for premium flower, potent concentrates, flavorful vapes, or infused pre-rolls, our Kearny Mesa location offers access to brands that have earned the trust of cannabis consumers across the state.

Each brand brings something unique to the table, giving customers a wide variety of options based on their preferences, experience level, and desired effects.

STIIIZY

STIIIZY is one of the most recognizable cannabis brands in California and has become a household name among cannabis enthusiasts. Known for its innovative products, premium genetics, and reliable quality, STIIIZY continues to be one of the most sought-after brands at Wellgreens Kearny Mesa.

Customers appreciate STIIIZY for its consistency, wide product selection, and ability to deliver exceptional cannabis experiences across multiple categories.

Popular STIIIZY products include:

  • Live Resin Pods
  • Premium Flower
  • STIIIZY 40’s Infused Pre-Rolls
  • All-In-One Vape Devices
  • Premium Vape Pods

Jeeter

Jeeter has built a reputation as one of the most popular infused pre-roll brands in the cannabis industry. Known for combining premium flowers with potent concentrates and flavorful terpenes, Jeeter products deliver a memorable experience that appeals to both casual and experienced consumers.

Its products are especially popular among customers looking for convenience without sacrificing quality.

Popular Jeeter products include:

  • Baby Jeeters
  • Jeeter XL Infused Pre-Rolls
  • Liquid Diamond Infused Joints
  • Multi-Pack Pre-Rolls

CBX (Cannabiotix)

CBX, short for Cannabiotix, is widely regarded as one of California’s premier indoor flower brands. Known for meticulous cultivation practices and award-winning genetics, CBX consistently produces some of the highest-quality flowers available on the market.

Cannabis connoisseurs often seek out CBX for its exceptional terpene profiles, visual appeal, and premium smoking experience.

Top CBX products include:

  • Kush Mountains
  • Cereal Milk
  • White Walker OG
  • Premium Indoor Flower Eighths
  • Limited-Release Strains

Legacy Genetix

Legacy Genetix has developed a loyal following among cannabis enthusiasts who appreciate rare genetics and exclusive cultivars. The brand focuses on producing small-batch flowers that highlight unique flavors, aromas, and effects.

Customers seeking something different often gravitate toward Legacy Genetix because of its focus on innovation and limited releases.

Customers often seek out:

  • Small-Batch Flower
  • Rare Hybrid Strains
  • Premium Indoor Cultivars
  • Exclusive Genetic Releases

Flav

Flav has become a favorite among consumers who value bold flavors and terpene-rich cannabis products. The brand focuses on delivering flavorful experiences while maintaining quality and consistency.

Whether you’re looking for premium flowers or convenient pre-rolls, Flav products are designed to showcase the natural characteristics of each strain.

Popular options include:

  • Premium Flower Eighths
  • Indoor Hybrid Strains
  • Signature Pre-Rolls
  • Limited Flower Releases

Lights Out

Lights Out has become a go-to brand for consumers seeking potent flower and deeply relaxing cannabis experiences. Known for producing high-THC cultivars and indica-dominant strains, the brand appeals to customers looking to unwind after a long day.

Its products are frequently chosen by experienced consumers who prioritize potency and flavor.

Popular Lights Out products include:

  • Premium Flower
  • High-THC Strains
  • Indica-Dominant Cultivars
  • Specialty Indoor Flower

Hashish

Hashish specializes in concentrate products and solventless cannabis options crafted for flavor-focused consumers. The brand has gained recognition for delivering premium extracts that preserve the natural characteristics of the cannabis plant.

For concentrate enthusiasts, Hashish provides an elevated experience that emphasizes purity and quality.

Popular Hashish products include:

  • Premium Hash
  • Solventless Concentrates
  • Live Rosin
  • Artisan Extracts

Allswell

Allswell delivers dependable cannabis products at accessible price points, making it a popular choice for everyday shoppers. The brand proves that quality cannabis doesn’t always have to come with a premium price tag.

Customers looking for value, consistency, and reliability often turn to Allswell as a trusted option.

Popular Allswell products include:

  • Everyday Flower
  • Value Pre-Rolls
  • Affordable Vape Products
  • Budget-Friendly Cannabis Options

Together, these trusted brands help make Wellgreens Kearny Mesa a destination for cannabis consumers seeking quality, variety, and consistency. Whether you’re searching for premium indoor flowers, infused pre-rolls, potent concentrates, or convenient vape products, our carefully curated brand lineup ensures there’s something for every preference and experience level.
