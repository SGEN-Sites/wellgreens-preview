Big Pete’s Cannabis Cookies: What to Know Before You Buy

September 2, 2026 · 13 min read

On this page

  1. Detailed Breakdown
  2. Key Takeaways
  3. FAQs

Cannabis edibles have expanded far beyond gummies, chocolates, and suspiciously green brownies.

Big Pete’s cannabis cookies bring the category back to something familiar: classic baked cookies made with cannabis-infused butter.

Founded in Santa Cruz, Big Pete’s has built its reputation around traditional baked edibles rather than treating the cookie as little more than a delivery system for THC. Wellgreens describes the family-run company as using cannabutter infused from cannabis flower and trim rather than conventional distillate, while Big Pete’s emphasizes soft-baked textures and familiar flavors.

That makes these products noticeably different from many modern gummies and highly refined edibles.

But a cannabis cookie is still a THC edible.

Before choosing Big Pete’s at Wellgreens, check the THC per cookie, number of servings, flavor, ingredients, allergens, package size, and your personal edible tolerance. And don’t assume that because a cookie looks like an ordinary snack, it should be eaten like one.

TLDR Quick Guide

Here’s the quick version of Big Pete’s cannabis cookies:

  • Big Pete’s is a family-run California cannabis edibles brand from Santa Cruz.
  • The brand is particularly known for cannabis-infused cookies.
  • Big Pete’s uses traditional cannabutter rather than making its classic cookies around distillate.
  • Current classic cookie flavors include options such as Chocolate Chip, Peanut Butter, and Cinnamon & Sugar.
  • Standard cookie products commonly contain 10mg THC per cookie, but always check the exact package.
  • Products may be sold individually or in multi-cookie packages depending on the item and retailer.
  • Ingredients can include common allergens such as wheat, milk, eggs, peanuts, and other ingredients depending on flavor.
  • Edibles generally take longer to produce noticeable effects than inhaled cannabis.
  • Don’t immediately consume more just because the first serving hasn’t taken effect.
  • Store cannabis cookies securely away from children and pets.

You can compare other Wellgreens cannabis brands or check in-stock cannabis products before shopping.

Detailed Breakdown

What Are Big Pete’s Cannabis Cookies?

Big Pete’s Treats is a California cannabis edibles company based in Santa Cruz.

Wellgreens traces the brand’s roots to a family cookie business founded by Pete Feurtado Sr., with cannabis-infused baked goods eventually becoming the company’s signature category. The current Wellgreens brand page describes Big Pete’s as a family-made baked edible producer centered on traditional cannabutter.

Instead of putting all the emphasis on candy-like flavor systems or high-tech formulations, Big Pete’s takes a more bakery-oriented approach.

Think:

classic cookie + cannabutter + measured THC.

Simple idea.

Considerably more important label.

What Makes Big Pete’s Different From Many Other Edibles?

The most important difference is the cannabutter.

Many THC edibles use distillate or another cannabis extract that’s incorporated into the recipe.

Big Pete’s instead highlights cannabis-infused butter as the foundation of its classic baked products. The company says its cannabutter helps distribute cannabis throughout the batter while preserving the familiar buttery texture expected from a cookie.

For shoppers, that means these aren’t simply regular cookies with a drop of flavorless THC concentrate added at the end.

The infusion method is part of the product’s identity.

What Is Cannabutter?

Cannabutter is butter infused with cannabis compounds.

In commercial regulated products, the objective is to create a consistent ingredient that can be incorporated throughout a recipe so servings contain predictable cannabinoid amounts.

Big Pete’s says its traditional cannabutter is infused using cannabis flower and trim rather than distillate.

That distinction may appeal to consumers who prefer a more traditional cannabis-edible approach.

It doesn’t mean you should estimate potency by taste, smell, or cookie size.

The label is still what matters.

What Flavors Are Available?

Big Pete’s has produced several classic cookie flavors over the years.

Current official product materials include staples such as:

  • Chocolate Chip
  • Peanut Butter
  • Cinnamon & Sugar

The broader product family has also included other limited, specialty, and higher-potency releases.

Wellgreens’ current Big Pete’s selection can change with inventory, so a flavor you’ve seen online isn’t guaranteed to be stocked at every location today.

Check in-stock cannabis products before making a special trip for one specific cookie.

Chocolate Chip: The Familiar Starting Point

Chocolate chip is about as classic as cookies get.

Big Pete’s current Chocolate Chip cookie materials list familiar bakery ingredients including chocolate chips, flour, butter, brown sugar, eggs, sugar, and cannabis. The standard product information identifies 10mg THC per cookie.

That familiar flavor profile can make the product approachable.

But familiarity should not be confused with low potency.

A 10mg THC cannabis cookie is still a measured cannabis edible, no matter how convincingly it resembles something you’d find next to a glass of milk.

Peanut Butter: Check the Allergen List

Big Pete’s Peanut Butter cookie is another classic flavor, but this one highlights why ingredient labels matter.

The official product information lists peanut butter, wheat flour, butter, eggs, and other ingredients, along with 10mg THC per cookie.

Anyone with food allergies or dietary restrictions should review the actual package before purchase.

Don’t assume that because two products come from the same cannabis brand, their ingredient and allergen profiles are identical.

They’re cookies.

The recipe matters.

Cinnamon & Sugar: Simple Flavor, Same Dosing Attention

Big Pete’s Cinnamon & Sugar product takes the snickerdoodle-style route.

Official product information lists ingredients such as wheat flour, sugar, butter, eggs, cinnamon, and cannabis, again with a standard 10mg THC serving per cookie in the current product documentation.

This is another good reminder that flavor preference and potency are separate decisions.

Choose the cookie flavor you enjoy.

Then independently decide whether its cannabinoid serving size suits your experience.

How Much THC Is in a Big Pete’s Cookie?

Current standard product sheets for Chocolate Chip, Peanut Butter, and Cinnamon & Sugar cookies list 10mg THC per cookie.

However, Big Pete’s has produced multiple product formats and potency levels over time.

Wellgreens notes that the broader brand has offered formats ranging from standard 10mg minis to much stronger specialty products.

That’s why the correct question isn’t:

“How strong are Big Pete’s cookies?”

It’s:

“How much THC is in this specific Big Pete’s product?”

Always read the package you’re actually purchasing.

Is 10mg THC a Beginner Dose?

There isn’t one universal edible dose that works the same way for everyone.

THC sensitivity can vary based on factors including:

  • Previous cannabis experience
  • Personal tolerance
  • Metabolism
  • Body response
  • Food intake
  • Other substances
  • Product formulation

For someone with little edible experience, 10mg can be considerably more THC than expected.

If you need a deeper primer before choosing an edible, Wellgreens’ edible dosage guide explains how serving sizes and tolerance fit into the decision.

The important principle is simple:

Don’t use somebody else’s normal dose as your personal measuring stick.

Why Edibles Require Patience

A pre-roll or vape produces effects relatively quickly because cannabinoids are inhaled.

A swallowed edible takes a different route through the body.

That means the onset can be much slower.

Big Pete’s current product sheets advise allowing approximately 30 minutes to two hours for the full effect of a standard cookie.

Individual timing can still vary.

This delay is exactly why edible overconsumption happens so easily.

The Classic Edible Mistake

It usually goes something like this:

Minute 30: “Nothing.”

Minute 45: “Still nothing.”

Minute 46: “Clearly I need another cookie.”

And then the first serving clocks in.

Follow the package instructions and allow sufficient time rather than immediately increasing the amount.

Cannabis edibles reward patience far more consistently than impatience.

One Cookie Isn’t Necessarily One Appropriate Serving for Everyone

Packaging can describe one cookie as a full-strength serving.

That doesn’t mean every consumer should automatically consume the entire piece.

People with lower tolerance may choose to consider smaller amounts where the product can be portioned accurately and the label permits it.

The package’s THC information should guide the decision.

Never eyeball an unlabeled cannabis cookie and try to estimate its potency from size.

Cookies are terrible measuring instruments.

Standard Cookies vs High-Potency Products

Big Pete’s broader brand history includes more than one potency format.

Wellgreens notes that the company has produced everything from standard 10mg products to much stronger specialty formats.

That makes packaging particularly important.

A familiar brand logo does not mean every product contains the same amount of THC.

Before eating anything, check:

  • THC per serving
  • THC per cookie
  • THC per package
  • Number of servings

Those numbers can be very different.

Why THC Per Package Matters

Suppose a package contains ten cookies at 10mg THC each.

That’s:

10mg per cookie × 10 cookies = 100mg THC in the entire package.

The package total isn’t a recommendation to consume all of it.

It tells you how much THC is contained in the complete product.

Read both the serving information and total cannabinoid content.

Big Pete’s Cannabis Cookies vs Gummies

Cookies and gummies are both edibles, but their formulations and eating experience differ considerably.

Cookies offer:

  • Baked texture
  • Butter-based recipes
  • Familiar dessert flavors
  • Larger physical serving size
  • Traditional bakery ingredients

Gummies generally offer:

  • Smaller pieces
  • Fruit flavors
  • Different formulations
  • Easier physical portioning in many products
  • Different ingredients and textures

Neither format is inherently better.

If you’re comparing baked edibles with gummies, Wellgreens’ Camino Edibles Guide provides a useful look at a very different cannabis edible style.

Cannabutter vs Distillate Gummies

This is where product philosophy becomes more noticeable.

Big Pete’s classic cookies emphasize flower-based cannabutter.

Many gummies across the industry use refined cannabis extracts such as distillate, though exact formulations vary by brand.

Some consumers enjoy the more traditional cannabis-edible character of cannabutter.

Others prefer gummies designed to minimize cannabis flavor.

Preference matters more than declaring one extraction approach universally superior.

Do Big Pete’s Cookies Taste Like Cannabis?

Cannabutter-based edibles can retain some cannabis character depending on the formulation and consumer’s palate.

But the cookies are also made around strong traditional flavors such as chocolate chip, peanut butter, cinnamon, and sugar.

How noticeable the cannabis taste seems can vary from person to person.

Don’t use flavor intensity to estimate potency.

A cookie that tastes strongly of cannabis isn’t automatically more potent, and a cookie that tastes mostly like dessert isn’t automatically weaker.

Ingredients Matter

Edibles are food products.

That sounds painfully obvious, yet shoppers often examine THC percentages much more carefully than ingredient lists.

With Big Pete’s cannabis cookies, potential ingredients can include:

  • Wheat
  • Milk
  • Eggs
  • Peanuts
  • Chocolate
  • Sugar
  • Oils
  • Flavorings
  • Cannabis

The exact list depends on the flavor.

If you have allergies, sensitivities, or dietary restrictions, check the specific package.

Don’t Assume All Cannabis Edibles Are Vegan or Gluten-Free

Big Pete’s now produces multiple edible categories, including some products with different dietary characteristics.

That does not mean the classic cookies automatically share those traits.

The official cookie product sheets include ingredients such as wheat flour, butter, and eggs in several classic formulations.

Read the cookie label rather than borrowing assumptions from another Big Pete’s product category.

Same brand.

Different recipe.

Freshness Matters With Baked Edibles

Cannabis cookies are still cookies.

Texture and flavor can change with age and storage.

Follow the package instructions and pay attention to:

  • Packaging integrity
  • Storage directions
  • Product dates where provided
  • Heat exposure
  • Moisture
  • Product condition

Proper storage also helps keep cannabis edibles clearly separated from ordinary snacks.

Secure Storage Is Essential

This is especially important with products that strongly resemble regular baked goods.

Store cannabis cookies:

  • In their original packaging when practical
  • Securely away from children
  • Away from pets
  • Separate from ordinary cookies
  • Where unauthorized users cannot access them

Don’t move THC cookies into an unlabeled kitchen container.

Nobody wants the phrase “Which cookies?” to become an emergency.

Don’t Drive While Impaired

THC can impair reaction time, judgment, and driving ability.

Don’t drive or operate machinery while impaired by Big Pete’s cannabis cookies or other THC products.

Edible effects can also last longer than effects from inhaled cannabis.

Plan transportation before consuming.

Not after.

Be Careful Combining THC With Alcohol

Combining intoxicating substances can increase impairment and make the experience harder to predict.

If you’re using THC edibles, be cautious about consuming alcohol or other intoxicants at the same time.

People taking medications or managing health conditions should seek appropriate professional guidance regarding potential interactions.

A cookie tasting like dessert doesn’t turn THC into an ordinary snack ingredient.

How to Compare Big Pete’s With Other Edible Brands

Wellgreens carries numerous cannabis brands, so Big Pete’s isn’t your only edible option.

When comparing products, look at:

  1. THC per serving
  2. Total THC per package
  3. Edible format
  4. Ingredients
  5. Allergens
  6. Flavor
  7. Cannabinoid formulation
  8. Current price
  9. Your tolerance

That makes the decision much more useful than asking which edible brand has the loudest packaging.

Before buying Big Pete’s cannabis cookies, read the product carefully.

How many milligrams are in one piece?

How much THC is in the entire package?

It can prevent considerably longer confusion later.

Are Big Pete’s Cannabis Cookies Worth Trying?

They make the most sense for adult cannabis consumers who specifically enjoy traditional baked edibles and cannabutter-based formulations.

If you prefer:

  • Familiar cookie flavors
  • Soft-baked edible formats
  • Cannabutter instead of conventional distillate
  • Clearly labeled THC servings
  • A long-running California edible brand

Big Pete’s is worth comparing with other regulated edible options.

If you prefer gummies, vegan formulations, smaller pieces, or another cannabinoid profile, a different edible may suit you better.

The “best edible” isn’t a universal product.

It’s the one whose format, potency, ingredients, and serving size match your preferences and tolerance.
