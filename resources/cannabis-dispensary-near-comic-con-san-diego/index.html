How to Plan Your Cannabis Shopping During Comic-Con

A little preparation can make your San Diego Comic-Con experience much easier. With thousands of visitors arriving in the city, popular products, busy schedules, and increased demand can make planning ahead especially important.

Instead of waiting until the last minute, visitors can take a few simple steps to make their cannabis shopping experience smoother and more convenient. Whether you are visiting San Diego for the first time or attending Comic-Con as a returning fan, knowing where to shop, what products are available, and what requirements are needed can save time during a busy convention weekend.

Before visiting a dispensary, consider the following:

Check Product Availability Before You Visit

Popular cannabis products and brands can sell quickly during major events like Comic-Con. Reviewing available inventory ahead of time allows you to see what products are currently available and helps you plan your purchase before arriving.

Choose a Convenient Dispensary Location

Location matters, especially during Comic-Con weekend when downtown San Diego can become crowded with visitors, traffic, and event activities.

  • Where you are staying

Wellgreens offers multiple dispensary locations throughout San Diego, making it easier for visitors to find a store that fits their travel plans. Explore available Wellgreens dispensary locations to find the most convenient option.

Bring Required Identification

Licensed cannabis dispensaries in California follow strict purchasing requirements to ensure products are sold responsibly.

Before visiting a dispensary, make sure you have:

  • A valid government-issued photo ID
  • Proof that you meet California’s legal purchasing age requirements
  • Any required information needed for your purchase

Having your identification ready helps create a faster and smoother shopping experience once you arrive.

Follow Event Rules and Local Regulations

While cannabis can be purchased legally from licensed dispensaries in California, visitors should remember that cannabis products cannot be brought into Comic-Con venues or restricted event areas.

To enjoy your trip responsibly:

  • Do not bring cannabis products into Comic-Con locations
  • Follow all venue rules and event policies
  • Purchase only from licensed dispensaries
  • Consume responsibly in permitted locations

Understanding the rules before your visit helps prevent unnecessary issues and allows you to focus on enjoying the convention.
