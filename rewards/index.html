FAQ

Rewards and deals FAQ

01Does Wellgreens have a rewards program?

Yes. Join our rewards program to get exclusive deal notifications and early access to sales. Some offers require joining. For how the program works today, open the Wellgreens app or ask a budtender.

02Is the Wellgreens app free?

Yes. The app is free on both the App Store and Google Play, with no subscription or membership fee.

03Can I combine deals?

Most deals cannot be combined unless specifically stated, so check the terms for each deal. The 50% first-order code can't be combined with other offers.

04When do daily deals change?

Daily deals reset at midnight and are available while supplies last.

05How do the delivery codes work?

Select Delivery at checkout and use FIRSTDELIVERY for 40% off your first delivery or SECONDDELIVERY for 30% off your second. The codes are for eligible delivery orders only. Minimums and fees can vary by store and address, so confirm the discount and total before you place your order.

06Can promotions change?

Yes. Promotions are valid for a limited time and may be modified or canceled at any time without notice. Unless we say otherwise, they have no cash value and are offered while supplies last.
