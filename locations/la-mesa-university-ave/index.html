Frequently Asked Questions

Questions About La Mesa - University Ave

01Where is La Mesa - University Ave located?

We're at 7180 University Ave in La Mesa, at University Ave & Massachusetts. We're a 3-minute walk from Better Buzz, across from Midas, and just off the 94 Massachusetts exit; head north about 3 minutes. Locals may remember us as the old A1 Rental shop.

02What are the hours for La Mesa - University Ave?

We're open daily from 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM. Early bird specials with 15% off are available before 9 AM.

03What products is La Mesa - University Ave known for?

Vapes, edibles, gummies, and flower are our top categories. We carry UP, Lights Out (flower and edibles), City Roots, and Lost Farm. Popular strains include classic OGs, Gorilla Glue, Sour Diesel, Girl Scout Cookies, Blue Dream, and Pink Acai.

04Does La Mesa - University Ave have a smoke shop?

Yes! We have unbeatable-price smoke shop items: glass, pipes, cones, wraps, papers, butane, lighters, glass cleaner, and eye drops.

05Can I order online or use the app for pickup at La Mesa - University Ave?

Yes! Order through our website or the Wellgreens Dispensary app for pickup, with loyalty rewards and stacked discounts.

06What areas does La Mesa - University Ave serve?

We serve La Mesa, San Diego, Chula Vista, El Cajon, Spring Valley, Lakeside, Santee, Lemon Grove, National City, Tierrasanta, Mission Hills, Mount Helix, and the Grossmont / College Area, with strong student traffic from SDSU, Grossmont, and UCSD.

07Does La Mesa - University Ave have Spanish-speaking or bilingual staff?

Yes! Hablamos español. Our bilingual budtenders are happy to help you in English or Spanish.

08What deals, early-bird specials, or loyalty perks are available at La Mesa - University Ave?

Early bird 15% off before 9 AM, loyalty rewards, stacked discounts, and daily value buys.

09What parking or transit options should I know about for La Mesa - University Ave?

Neighborhood street parking and plaza access at University & Massachusetts; quick hop from the 94 Massachusetts exit.

10What makes La Mesa - University Ave stand out in its neighborhood?

Slower-paced, education-forward shopping: budtenders prioritize one-on-one guidance in a calm, organized showroom.
