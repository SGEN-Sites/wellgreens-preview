Frequently Asked Questions

Questions About Kearny Mesa

01Where is Kearny Mesa located?

We're at 8039 Balboa Ave Suite A in San Diego. You'll find us across from Kearny Mesa Dodge Chrysler Jeep Ram, north of In-N-Out Kearny Mesa, and east of the Aston Martin dealership. Take the east exit of 163, east of 805.

02What are the hours for Kearny Mesa?

We're open daily from 6:00 AM to 9:45 PM. We have early bird specials with 15% off before 9 AM, plus an exclusive afternoon happy hour Monday through Friday from 12 to 2 PM.

03What products is Kearny Mesa known for?

We're strong in vapes and cartridges, flower, and indica-leaning sleep products. We also carry Protabs tablets for precise dosing. Top brands include UP, City Roots, Allswell, Yada Yada, and Oakfruitland. Popular strains include Mob Ties, Gush Mints, No Breaks, and Blue Crack.

04Does Kearny Mesa have a smoke shop?

Yes! Full smoke shop and accessories including glass, pipes, hardware, batteries, devices, cones, and wraps.

05Can I order online or use the app for pickup at Kearny Mesa?

Yes! Order online through our website or the Wellgreens Dispensary app for the fastest pickup: built for commuters and lunch-hour visits.

06What areas does Kearny Mesa serve?

We serve Kearny Mesa, Clairemont, Serra Mesa, Tierrasanta, Mira Mesa, and greater central San Diego along the 163/805 corridor.

07Does Kearny Mesa have Spanish-speaking or bilingual staff?

Yes! Hablamos español. Our bilingual budtenders can assist you with products, effects, and deals in both English and Spanish.

08What deals, early-bird specials, or loyalty perks are available at Kearny Mesa?

Early bird 15% off before 9 AM; exclusive afternoon happy hour 15% off Monday-Friday 12-2 PM; loyalty rewards and stacked discounts.

09What parking or transit options should I know about for Kearny Mesa?

Balboa Ave retail corridor with on-site parking; across from Kearny Mesa Dodge and north of In-N-Out, easy in-and-out.

10What makes Kearny Mesa stand out in its neighborhood?

Self-serve floor tuned for quick visits between jobs or commutes: strong vape, flower, and sleep products with minimal friction.
