Frequently Asked Questions

Questions About Encinitas

01Where is Encinitas located?

We're at 211 N El Camino Real in Encinitas, across from the Bev · Mo / Rubio's plaza and next to Buggy Bath Car Wash. We're less than a quarter mile from the Sheriff's office and just a few miles from the beach.

02What are the hours for Encinitas?

We're open daily from 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM. Early bird specials with 15% off are available before 9 AM.

03What products is Encinitas known for?

We're wellness-heavy and CBD-forward, with strong selections in edibles, sleep aids, low-THC and high-CBD products, pain rubs, topicals, and tinctures. Top brands include CBX, Claybourne, Blem, Lights Out, 710 Labs, and Jetty. We also carry sativa flower for daytime and creative use.

04Does Encinitas have a smoke shop?

Yes! Despite our boutique atmosphere, we carry a full smoke shop selection: bongs, pipes, glass, cones, wraps, papers, butane, and lighters. Boutique meets one-stop convenience.

05Can I order online or use the app for pickup at Encinitas?

Yes! Order through our website or the Wellgreens Dispensary app for quick pickup, with loyalty rewards and stacked discounts.

06What areas does Encinitas serve?

We serve Encinitas, Carlsbad, Oceanside, Escondido, Vista, San Marcos, Rancho Santa Fe, Solana Beach, and Cardiff-by-the-Sea. We're one of only three dispensaries in Encinitas.

07Does Encinitas have Spanish-speaking or bilingual staff?

Yes! Hablamos español. Our bilingual staff can walk you through wellness products, effects, and deals in English and Spanish.

08What deals, early-bird specials, or loyalty perks are available at Encinitas?

Early bird 15% off before 9 AM, loyalty rewards, stacked discounts, and bulk options for longer stays when available.

09What parking or transit options should I know about for Encinitas?

We have the largest dispensary parking lot in Encinitas; across from Bev · Mo / Rubio's and minutes from the coast.

10What makes Encinitas stand out in its neighborhood?

Calm, clean environment tailored to first-timers and wellness shoppers: expert guidance on tinctures, topicals, and balanced THC:CBD ratios.
